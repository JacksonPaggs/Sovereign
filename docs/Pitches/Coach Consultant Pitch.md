"The problem with most coach websites is they're either too generic — stock photos, bullet points, no personality — or they're trying to do too much. Neither converts. People buy you, not a template."

*(Show the Sovereign site)*

"This is the level we build at. Your brand, your voice, designed to get people to book a call the moment they land on it."

"For you specifically — a site built around your personal brand, with a clear path from landing to booking, and copy that actually sounds like you."

## PRICING

"We're building out our portfolio in the coaching space so we're offering an intro rate for the first few projects."

## OBJECTIONS

*"I already have clients"*
"Great — imagine if your site was working while you slept. How many discovery calls are you booking from people who found you cold? A good site multiplies everything you're already doing."

*"I'm not sure my audience is online"*
"If they have a phone, they're online. And they're Googling you before they buy from you regardless of how they found you."

*"I handle my own marketing"*
"This isn't marketing — it's your foundation. Everything you're doing drives people to your site eventually. If the site doesn't convert, you're losing the work you've already done."

---

## THE CLOSE

"Let me put together a quick mockup around your brand — no charge, no commitment. You see it, you love it, we move. You don't, no hard feelings. Fair?"

---

## AFTER THEY SAY YES

- Get headshot and brand colours
- Get their core offer and target client description
- Get testimonials if available
- Get Calendly or booking link
- Get their story — what makes them different
- Turnaround: 5–7 business days
- Ask for testimonial + permission to use as case study
