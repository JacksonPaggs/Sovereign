"We're expanding into the home services space right now so we're taking on a few projects at an intro rate before we go to full pricing."

## OBJECTIONS

*"I get all my work through word of mouth"*
"That's great — but every one of those referrals is going to Google you before they call. If your site doesn't back up what they heard, you lose them. A good site makes word of mouth close faster."

*"I tried a website before and it didn't do anything"*
"That's because most web designers build something that looks okay but isn't built to convert. What we build is specifically designed to turn visitors into calls and quote requests."

*"Too expensive"*
"One job covers it. What's your average project value?"

---

## THE CLOSE

"Let me put together a quick mockup — no charge, no commitment. If you like it we move forward, if not you've lost nothing. Sound good?"

---

## AFTER THEY SAY YES

- Get logo and brand colours (or build from scratch)
- Get photos of completed jobs
- Get list of services and service areas
- Get phone number and preferred contact method
- Turnaround: 5–7 business days
- Ask for testimonial + permission to use as case study
