# Sovereign — Med Spa & Aesthetics Pitch

---

## COLD OPENING MESSAGE

"Hey [Name] — I came across [Business Name] and love what you're doing. I noticed your website doesn't quite match the premium experience you're clearly offering in person. I build custom sites for aesthetics clinics and just finished something I think you'd want to see. Mind if I send it over?"

---

## FOLLOW-UP (No response after 2 days)

"Hey [Name] — just circling back. I work with med spas in the Salt Lake area and I think there's a real opportunity to make your online presence match the quality of your services. Takes 2 minutes to look at what we do. Worth it?"

---

## ONCE THEY'RE INTERESTED

"The issue with most med spa websites is they look clinical or outdated — neither of which matches what you're actually selling, which is a premium, transformative experience. Your site should make someone feel that before they ever walk in."

*(Show the Sovereign site)*

"This is the level we build at. Custom, premium, built to convert browsers into bookings."

"What I'd build for you — a site that reflects the quality of your clinic, with online booking built in, before/after gallery, and service pages that actually sell. Done in under 2 weeks."

---

## PRICING

$1,500–2,000 flat for first clients.

"We're building our portfolio in the aesthetics space right now, so we're offering an introductory rate. After our first few clinics, pricing goes to standard."

---

## OBJECTIONS

*"We already have a website"*
"I know — I looked at it. The question is whether it's converting. How many new clients would you say come in specifically because they found you online and loved what they saw?"

*"We get clients through referrals"*
"Referrals are great — but imagine if every referral landed on a site that immediately validated everything they heard about you. Referrals close faster when the site backs it up."

*"Too expensive"*
"One new client covers the cost. What's your average transaction value per client?"

*"I need to think about it"*
"What's the main hesitation? I'd rather answer it now."

---

## THE CLOSE

"Here's what I'll do — let me put together a quick mockup using your branding, no charge, no commitment. You see it, you love it, we move forward. You don't, you've lost nothing. Fair?"

---

## AFTER THEY SAY YES

- Get logo, brand colours, photos of the clinic
- Get list of services with descriptions
- Get booking link (Vagaro, MindBody, Calendly, etc.)
- Get 3 before/after photos if available
- Turnaround: 5–7 business days
- Ask for testimonial + permission to use as case study
