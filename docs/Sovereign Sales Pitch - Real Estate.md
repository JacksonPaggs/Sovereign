# Sovereign — Real Estate Agent Pitch

---

## 1. COLD OPENING MESSAGE (Text / DM / Email)

> "Hey [Name] — I was looking at your website and I think there's a real opportunity to make it work harder for you. I run a digital agency and just built something I think you'd want to see. Mind if I send it over? No pitch, just want to show you what's possible."

**Rules:**
- Personalise the name, always
- Don't mention price
- Don't say "I'd love to help you" — too salesy
- End with a yes/no question to get a reply

---

## 2. FOLLOW-UP (If No Response After 2 Days)

> "Hey [Name] — just following up. I build websites for real estate agents in the Salt Lake area and wanted to show you a quick example of what we do. Takes 2 minutes to look at. Worth it?"

---

## 3. THE CONVERSATION (Once They're Interested)

### Open
> "So I looked at your current site — the main issue is it's not converting. Someone lands on it, they don't immediately feel like you're the person they want to work with. That's fixable."

### Show the Sovereign site
> "This is the level we build at. Custom, premium, built to make whoever sees it feel like they're working with someone serious."

### The ask
> "Here's what I'd do for you — a full custom site, your brand, your listings front and centre, Calendly booking built in so people can go straight from your site to a call with you. Done in under 2 weeks."

---

## 4. PRICING

**First client:** $1,500–2,000 flat
*(Position as a limited introductory rate, not a discount)*

> "We're taking on a small number of first projects at a reduced rate while we build out our portfolio in the real estate space. After that we go to standard pricing. So the timing is good for you."

---

## 5. OBJECTION HANDLING

**"I already have a website"**
> "I know — I looked at it. The question isn't whether you have one, it's whether it's winning you clients. If you closed one extra deal from your website in the next 6 months, what's that worth to you?"

**"I need to think about it"**
> "Totally fair. What's the main thing you'd want to think through? I'd rather answer it now than have you sitting on a question."

**"It's too expensive"**
> "I hear you. What's your budget looking like? I want to find something that works — and even at the lower end, what we build is going to outperform what you have."

**"I don't have time right now"**
> "You don't have to do anything — that's the point. I handle everything. I just need about an hour of your time total to get what I need, and you get a site that works while you're focused on closing deals."

---

## 6. THE CLOSE

> "Here's what I'd suggest — let me put together a quick mockup for your brand, no charge, no commitment. If you love it we move forward, if not you've lost nothing. Sound fair?"

---

## 7. AFTER THEY SAY YES

- Get their logo, brand colours, headshot
- Get 3 sentences about what makes them different
- Get their Calendly or booking link
- Turnaround: 5–7 business days
- Ask for a testimonial and before/after permission on delivery

---

## NOTES
- Always lead with what's wrong with their current site, not what you offer
- The ROI conversation is easy: one extra closed deal at $10k+ commission = site pays for itself 5x over
- Draper / Salt Lake market is strong — use local credibility ("I'm based in Draper")
- Don't oversell. Show the site, let it speak.
